# Community

Join the G3W-SUITE community to ask questions, share ideas and help improve the project.

## Questions and discussion

Use GitHub Discussions to ask questions, exchange ideas and share how you use G3W-SUITE.

[Join the discussion](https://github.com/g3w-suite/community/discussions "primary-button")

## Contribute to G3W-SUITE

Explore the source code, report issues and contribute to the project.

[Explore G3W-SUITE on GitHub](https://github.com/g3w-suite "outline-button")

## Follow the roadmap

Track planned work and follow ongoing development on the public roadmap.

[View the public roadmap](https://github.com/orgs/g3w-suite/projects/12 "outline-button")
