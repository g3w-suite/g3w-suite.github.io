# Publish a WebGIS service

## Access the online service

To publish the project, you can **access the G3W-SUITE test application** via the following URL: [**`https://v311.g3wsuite.it`**](https://v311.g3wsuite.it)

Log in to the **Administration Panel** with these credentials:
* user: **`demo`**
* password: **`G3wsuite123`**
 
**In case of login fails, report the problem to `info@gis3w.it`**

## Publish the QGIS project as a WebGis service

After logging in, open the **`Administration session`** to view the **Dashboard**.

In the Dashboard there will be the menu for access to the **Cartographic Groups** pertaining to your user and the menus relating to additional modules not active in this demo.

Click on the **`Show`** item in the ligth blue **Groups** box to acces at the list of available Cartographic Groups.

![Screenshot: Demo Dashboard](/img/manual-english-demo-dashboard.avif)

Access the list of webgis in the Cartographic Group **`Demo Group`** clicking on the project number shown.

![Screenshot: Demo Choose Group](/img/manual-english-demo-choose-group.avif)

![Screenshot: Demo Project Manage](/img/manual-english-demo-project-manage.avif)

Now click on the button ![Button: Add QGIS Project](/img/manual-english-button-add-qgis-project.avif) to publish your **QGIS project**.

In **QGIS file**, select the `.qgs` or `.qgz` project you prepared in [Download demo data](demo.md#download-demo-data).

For this exercise, keep the default options unless you want to test a specific setting. See [Publish and update WebGIS services](publish-and-update-webgis-services.md#publication-of-new-webgis-services) for the full explanation of permissions, base layers, and publication options.

After filling in the various form, click on the **`Save button`** to confirm your choices.

![Button: Save](/img/manual-english-buttom-save.avif)

**If the publication was successful, the QGIS project will appear in the list of projects in the Cartographic Group.**

![Screenshot: Iconsmall Viewmap](/img/manual-english-iconsmall-viewmap.avif) Clicking on the **`View map`** icon will access the WebGis service in consultation.

![Screenshot: Demo Webgis Project](/img/manual-english-demo-webgis-project.avif)

## Update the published WebGis service

If you want to modify some graphic-functional aspects of your WebGis service, modify your QGIS project and update the WebGis service by clicking on the **`Modify`** icon ![Screenshot: Iconsmall Edit](/img/manual-english-iconsmall-edit.avif).

Reupload the QGIS project with your changes, click on the **Save button** and see the resuts on the cartographic client.
