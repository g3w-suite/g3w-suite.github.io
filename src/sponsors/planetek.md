# Planetek Italia

![Planetek Italia](/img/site-planetek.avif)

Planetek Italia works with geospatial information and Earth observation for applications including environmental and infrastructure monitoring.

Its activities also include space missions and services for monitoring changes in the Earth's surface.

Founded in 1994, Planetek Italia is a benefit company specialising in geomatics, Earth science and software for space missions. Its work covers the full life cycle of geospatial information: acquiring data, storing and managing it, analysing it and making the results available to others.

The company processes satellite, aerial and drone observations to produce maps and geographic information. Its services include the continuous monitoring of land, infrastructure, construction sites, urban change and coastal or marine areas. These applications use observations collected over time to support operational activities and decision-making.

Planetek also designs spatial data infrastructures for storing, managing and sharing geographic datasets. Alongside Earth observation, it develops applications based on real-time positioning and software for processing data and images on board satellites and within ground infrastructure. Its work therefore spans both the information gathered from space and the systems used to handle it.

The fields described on its website include environmental and territorial monitoring, public administration, engineering, transport, utilities and energy. Scientific satellite missions and space exploration are another part of its activities. Planetek Italia is part of the D-Orbit group and works in national and international markets as well as research.

Its solutions include the processing of imagery for cartography, services based on satellite observations and infrastructures for sharing spatial information. The company also works with positioning systems such as GPS, Galileo and other GNSS technologies, connecting geographic data with real-time location.

Planetek Italia has supported four releases of G3W-SUITE. Its official website provides more information about its services and projects.
