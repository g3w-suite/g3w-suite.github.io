# Regione Lazio

![Regione Lazio](/img/site-logo-regione-lazio.avif)

Supported releases:

[3.2](/en/news/v3.2/) · [3.5](/en/news/v3.5/)

Regione Lazio supported G3W-SUITE versions 3.2 and 3.5. Version 3.2 brought compatibility with QGIS 3.16 LTR and extended the integration of DataPlotly charts. It also added options for filtering attribute tables and using selections to control the content shown on maps and charts.

Version 3.5 expanded editing tools and the handling of raster and vector time series. Its features included working with overlapping geometries, copying between layers and displaying default values in editing forms. A summary of WebGIS settings made it easier to review searches and editing permissions for a published service.

Version 3.2 added QGIS 3.16 LTR compatibility and improved DataPlotly integration.

The region includes Rome and surrounding provinces. Its institutional website publishes information and services for residents, including civil protection bulletins and alerts.

Lazio is a region of central Italy that includes the capital and a varied territory extending from the Tyrrhenian coast inland. The regional administration works across this territory and provides public information and services through its institutional website.

The website brings together news and access to regional services. Among the information available to residents are civil protection bulletins and alerts, alongside updates on public health, regional initiatives and other areas of administration. These resources connect regional decisions and services with the communities they concern.

Geographic information can describe a region at several scales, from individual places to connections across municipalities and provinces. Web maps provide a way to explore this information through layers, searches and links to related data, making a territorial view available alongside other public resources.
