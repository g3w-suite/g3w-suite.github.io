# ARPA Piemonte

![ARPA Piemonte](/img/site-arpa-piemonte.avif)

ARPA Piemonte is the regional agency for environmental protection in Piedmont, Italy.

The agency supported G3W-SUITE versions 3.4, 3.5 and 3.7. Version 3.4 introduced conditional forms and raster and vector time-series visualisation; version 3.5 expanded editing and time-series tools; version 3.7 integrated QGIS analysis models, saved layer filters and multi-layer editing permissions.

Version 3.7 added integration of geographic analysis models created in QGIS, along with tools for saved layer filters and managing editing permissions on multiple layers.

Its environmental information covers topics from air and water to climate and natural hazards. The agency also publishes weather and hydrogeological alerts for the region.

ARPA Piemonte brings together environmental information across the region, with material organised both by subject and by province. Its website covers Alessandria, Asti, Biella, Cuneo, Novara, Torino, Verbania and Vercelli, allowing visitors to explore information in a territorial context.

The subjects presented include water, air, climate, geology and natural instability, weather, snow and glaciers. The agency also provides information on noise, electromagnetic fields, radioactivity, industrial risk, soil and waste. These areas reflect the variety of environmental questions that arise across Piedmont, from urban areas to the Alps.

Environmental monitoring is accompanied by public communication. The agency publishes environmental news, practical information and answers to questions about issues such as noise, asbestos and electromagnetic fields. It also offers educational resources on sustainability and makes meteorological and hydrogeological alerts accessible from its website.
