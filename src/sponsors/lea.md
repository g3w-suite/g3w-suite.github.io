# LEA

![LEA](/img/site-lea-logo.avif)

LEA provides utility services in the Locarno area of Switzerland, including drinking water.

Its website offers customer services such as requests for network connections, meter readings and fault reports. It also publishes updates about the water supply.

LEA stands for Locarnese Ente Acqua. It provides drinking-water services in Locarno, Losone and Muralto, in the Swiss canton of Ticino. Its work concerns the water network and the service that connects the supply to homes and other properties in the area.

The organisation's website brings together information for customers and residents. It provides forms for requesting a connection to the network, reporting a change of property ownership and submitting meter readings. A separate service allows users to report a fault, with details such as the municipality, address and type of problem.

For a new connection, LEA makes a downloadable application form available online. The completed and signed request is submitted before work begins. These practical services sit alongside news about the water supply, giving residents a place to find both administrative information and updates affecting their area.

LEA also publishes information when incidents affect the network. Its news section has covered interruptions caused by damage to a drinking-water pipeline and subsequent updates on the restoration of potable water. Such notices connect the day-to-day service with the infrastructure that makes water distribution possible.

In 2024, LEA also took on the next stages of a planned thermal network for Locarno's old town. The proposal uses groundwater as a means of heating or cooling buildings, offering an alternative to older oil-fired systems in the historic centre.

LEA supported G3W-SUITE versions 3.10 and 3.11. Version 3.10 introduced compatibility with QGIS 3.40 LTR, map annotations and an Elasticsearch-based search tool. Version 3.11 followed with compatibility for QGIS 3.44 LTR and further improvements to map interaction, editing and charts.

The release links below provide an overview of the platform's development. LEA's own website offers details of its water services, customer procedures and news for the Locarno area.
