# QTIBIA Engineering

![QTIBIA Engineering](/img/site-qtibia-long.avif)

QTIBIA Engineering shares QGIS and open-source GIS training and resources in Romania.

Its website features QGIS courses and tutorials, including guidance on installing QGIS and PostGIS on Windows, alongside articles about open-source software. Its training has also addressed water and wastewater utilities.

QTIBIA organises QGIS courses for people working with geographic information in Romania. Its course material addresses both beginners and more experienced users, combining foundations with practical exercises. Examples of the fields served include water and wastewater, urban planning, land records, green spaces, energy, transport and telecommunications.

The training is designed around the use of GIS in professional contexts. Participants can work through practical examples and bring questions from their own workplaces. The company's course information also describes QGIS certification for participants who complete its training.

QTIBIA's published resources extend beyond scheduled courses. Its website includes tutorials on installing QGIS and PostGIS on Windows and preparing a virtual machine, as well as articles about the open-source ecosystem. These resources connect desktop mapping with the databases and environments used to manage geographic information.

In 2020, QTIBIA worked with the Romanian QGIS user association on specialist training for water and wastewater organisations. The programme brought together topics such as databases, data standardisation, migration and exchange for GIS, IT, SCADA and hydraulic modelling teams. Participants practised in a virtual laboratory using open-source tools.

That laboratory combined Linux, PostgreSQL and PostGIS, QGIS, QGIS Server, QGIS Web Client and Grafana. The training addressed how different technical teams can work with data across connected systems, from geographic information to time series and operational workflows.

QTIBIA supported G3W-SUITE 3.7, a release that introduced the integration of analysis models created with QGIS Model Designer. It also added saved layer filters and tools for configuring editing permissions across multiple layers. The version link below provides the full changelog.
