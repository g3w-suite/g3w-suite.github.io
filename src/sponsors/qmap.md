# QMAP

![QMAP](/img/site-qmap-logo.png)

QMAP provides GIS services for territorial digitalization and management of geospatial data.

Its portal describes services for public administrations and businesses, including the conversion of planning documents into GIS data and the management of cadastral information.

QMAP's service catalogue focuses on making territorial information usable in digital form. It describes converting urban planning documents from CAD, PDF or analogue sources into georeferenced GIS data, with thematic layers and metadata. Its intended audiences include local public administrations as well as private organisations.

The catalogue also describes the management of street names and addresses, land and cadastral parcels, and local registers of green spaces. These are distinct categories of information that can be associated with places and maintained as part of a territorial database. QMAP presents tools for linking cadastral and planning information with other local records.

Other listed applications concern the location of built and natural heritage, the management of public and private investment information, agricultural records and planning certificates. Together, these examples illustrate the range of administrative subjects that may be represented in a geographic information system. Details of the available services are provided on QMAP's website.

The portal also presents tools for managing construction permits and recording the status of investment projects. Its catalogue brings planning, land records and local infrastructure together as related areas of territorial administration. Each service is described in relation to the data it helps maintain.

QMAP appears among the funders of G3W-SUITE 3.8, released in June 2024. That version included compatibility with QGIS 3.34 LTR, saved map themes for authenticated users and additional print formats. It also offered PDF downloads of attributes resulting from a query or search.

The same release brought changes to editing workflows involving 1:N relationships and the ability to begin editing from the attribute table. Discover these improvements and more in the changelog below.
