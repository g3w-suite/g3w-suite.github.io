# Comune di Trento

![Comune di Trento](/img/site-trento.avif)

The Municipality of Trento is the local government of Trento, in northern Italy.

Trento is the capital of the Autonomous Province of Trento. The municipality makes geographic information available through its online GIS portal, alongside its public services.

The city's geographic information service provides access to base maps of the municipal area. Its published datasets include the technical map, aerial photographs, street names, municipal property and the land cadastre. The portal describes these resources as a reference for more specialised cartographic themes.

According to the municipality, this geographic data was developed from the 1990s onwards and has been updated and expanded over time. Users can explore the information by navigating the map or search using identifiers such as a street and house number or cadastral municipality and parcel number. This offers different ways to locate information about the same territory.

The portal brings the technical map and aerial imagery together with place names and cadastral references. These base resources provide a shared geographic context for consulting more specialised information about the municipality.

The municipality's institutional website also provides information on local government, public works and services. Its administrative context brings together the city, its neighbourhoods and the surrounding municipal territory. The online GIS is one way of making territorial information available alongside those public resources.

The Municipality of Trento supported G3W-SUITE versions 3.3, 3.4 and 3.6. Together, these releases cover developments in access controls, editing forms, geographic search and map interaction.

Version 3.4 introduced cascading and conditional editing forms and tools for visualising raster and vector time series. Version 3.6 added multilayer search, spatial bookmarks and controls for layer transparency.

Discover the city's GIS portal among the examples of organisations using G3W-SUITE on this site.
