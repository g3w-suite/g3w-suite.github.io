# ARPA Liguria

![ARPA Liguria](/img/site-arpal.avif)

ARPA Liguria is the regional agency for environmental protection in Liguria, Italy.

Its work includes monitoring water resources and publishing weather forecasts and information on pollen and spores. These services make environmental information available across the region.

Established by regional legislation in 1995 and operational since 1998, ARPAL provides technical and scientific support to the regional government, provinces and municipalities. Its responsibilities bring together environmental protection, water resources, soil protection, civil protection and public health.

The agency monitors air quality, inland and marine waters, soil and natural hazards across Liguria. Its observation networks, field measurements and sampling activities help describe environmental conditions over time. This work also covers groundwater and landslides, linking environmental monitoring with the assessment of risks affecting the territory.

ARPAL's meteorological and hydrological centre produces forecasts, alerts and risk assessments in support of regional civil protection. The agency also works on marine issues and biodiversity, while its public website offers information on subjects such as bathing waters, weather, pollen and spores. These services bring different kinds of environmental observations together for a broad audience.

Environmental controls extend to industrial activities and technological risks. The agency carries out inspections and technical assessments and deals with subjects including noise, electromagnetic fields and radiation. Its regional laboratory analyses environmental samples as well as food and drinking water, using facilities distributed across Liguria's four provinces.

The regional laboratory carries out chemical, microbiological, biological and physical analyses. Its work supports environmental checks and public-health prevention, alongside the agency's field monitoring and technical assessments.

The agency's role combines monitoring, laboratory analysis and communication with support for public institutions. Its official website provides access to its environmental themes, services and organisational information.
