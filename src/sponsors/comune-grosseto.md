# Comune di Grosseto

![Comune di Grosseto](/img/site-grosseto-logo.png)

The Municipality of Grosseto is the local government of Grosseto, in Tuscany.

The municipality publishes information on local services and civil protection. Grosseto is the provincial capital and a centre of the Maremma area in southern Tuscany.

The municipal administration serves a territory in the Maremma, an area that includes the city and its surrounding landscape. Its institutional website brings together information about the municipality's offices, governing bodies, public documents and services, alongside local news and events.

The website's administration section describes the areas responsible for municipal services and provides access to documents, statistics, public notices and information about the council and executive. Residents can also find contacts for offices and practical information about the services offered by the municipality.

Civil protection is among the subjects covered by the municipality's public information. Its website publishes local notices about the municipal civil protection plan and weather-related alerts, connecting administrative information with the territory and the people who live there.

The Municipality of Grosseto supported G3W-SUITE versions 3.6 and 3.9. Version 3.6 introduced compatibility with QGIS 3.28 LTR, searches across multiple layers and spatial bookmarks. It also added controls for layer transparency and tools for returning to previous or subsequent map views.

Version 3.9 focused on the map client and editing configuration. It introduced a redesigned client, pagination of search and query results, and options for sharing editing settings between users. Its changes also addressed related records and the presentation of information in attribute tables.

Both releases form part of the development of an open-source platform for publishing QGIS projects as web maps. Their changelogs are linked below, while the municipality's website provides current information about Grosseto's services, administration and local initiatives.
