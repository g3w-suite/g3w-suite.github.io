# InterCad

![InterCad](/img/site-intercad.png)

InterCad is a Swiss technology company working with digital solutions for GIS and infrastructure; it is now part of the NTI Group.

The Swiss team also offers consulting, software and training for fields including architecture, construction and manufacturing.

Intercad has worked with digital tools for architecture, building information modelling and geographic information systems. In January 2026, Intercad and Hurni Engineering joined the NTI Group; the two Swiss businesses subsequently came together as NTI Hurni Engineering & Intercad. Their combined offer includes software, consulting, training, support and services.

The Swiss team describes its work across manufacturing, architecture, construction and infrastructure as well as GIS. Hurni Engineering contributes experience in design and product data and lifecycle management, while Intercad brings expertise in architecture, BIM and geographic information. The combined organisation connects local services with the wider NTI group.

Its website presents consulting, products and training as complementary services. The stated aim is to help organisations choose and implement digital tools and develop the skills needed to use them. The geographic information and infrastructure offering sits alongside services for other design and engineering disciplines.

The group's Swiss offering includes support throughout the adoption and everyday use of software. Training is available online and on site, while consulting covers the selection and implementation of solutions for different professional contexts.

InterCad supported G3W-SUITE 3.10. Released in July 2025, this version introduced compatibility with QGIS 3.40 LTR and changes to the cartographic client, including map annotations, map sharing, improvements to charts and an Elasticsearch-based search tool.

The version also included changes to editing and related records, and options to customise aspects of search and query results.

The team's website provides more information about its services and expertise.
