# Comune di Bari

![Comune di Bari](/img/site-bari-city-logo.avif)

The Municipality of Bari is the local government of Bari, in southern Italy.

The city is the capital of Apulia and lies on the Adriatic coast, where it has a major port. Its municipal administration serves the city and its residents through local public services.

Bari is both a coastal city and a regional centre, with a historic urban core and a territory that extends beyond the waterfront. The municipality's work concerns the city and its neighbourhoods, connecting local administration with services and information for residents.

The city's position on the Adriatic gives it a longstanding relationship with the sea and with transport through its port. Its streets, public spaces and surrounding districts form a diverse urban territory, where geographic information can provide a common reference for understanding places and their relationships.

As a local authority, the municipality works across different areas of public life, from local services to the management of the urban environment. Maps and territorial information offer ways to describe locations and bring different kinds of data together in a view of the city.

The Municipality of Bari supported G3W-SUITE versions 3.3 and 3.4. Version 3.3 introduced more detailed editing permissions for individual layers and users, as well as support for QGIS expressions in online editing. It also expanded tools for searching and consulting geographic information.

Version 3.4 continued this development with cascading and conditional editing forms, support for raster and vector time series, and additional map interaction options. The release also made it possible to start editing directly from query or search results and to measure geometry during editing.

G3W-SUITE uses QGIS projects as the basis for interactive maps published on the web. The links below provide the details of the two supported releases, while the municipality's official website offers information about the city, its administration and its services.
