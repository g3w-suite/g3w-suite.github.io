# Leonardo Lami (@leolami)

![Leonardo Lami](https://github.com/leolami.png?size=400)

**For Leonardo Lami (@leolami), the public trail leads to two foundations of a usable WebGIS: its server and the guides that help people use it.**

His public profile points to a [G3W-ADMIN repository](https://github.com/leolami/g3w-admin) and [end-user documentation](https://github.com/leolami/g3w-suite-documentation), whose description highlights editing and translation. Together, they sit on either side of the same job: publish a QGIS project, then help people understand how to use it.

## From platform to people

The public record for this account does not identify a standalone feature change to credit, so this page focuses on the work its repositories document: server-side publishing and the user guides around it.
