# Matteo Tosi

![Matteo Tosi](https://github.com/Raruto.png?size=400)

**Matteo Tosi builds the extensions that make maps feel alive and the plugin pathways that let G3W-SUITE grow.**

## Beyond G3W-SUITE

His Leaflet projects solve map problems directly: [leaflet-elevation](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation) draws elevation profiles with SVG and Canvas, [leaflet-rotate](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-rotate) rotates maps, and [leaflet-kmz](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-kmz) loads KMZ data. He also publishes technical notes on [Raruto](https://raruto.github.io/).

## In G3W-SUITE

Matteo works on the suite's extension points and release flow: plugin-author guidance and build automation in G3W-CLIENT, plus the move of G3W-ADMIN's editing tool to a static plugin and Sentry monitoring integration. [Plugin development guide](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/977) · [Editing plugin change](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin/pull/1184) · [Sentry integration](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin/commit/46f0567d).
