# G3W-SUITE team

![G3W-SUITE logo](/img/site-g3w-suite-logo-2024.png)

We work on G3W-SUITE, an open-source WebGIS project built around QGIS. Its administration server and map client help teams publish their projects, share geographic information and work with data in the browser.

Our project brings together developers, users, sponsors and contributors. Code, documentation, translations, testing and feedback all play a part in making the tools useful in different places and for different kinds of work.

Gis3W is the project's main sponsor and offers consulting, support and development around the platform. Other organizations support individual releases, while the public repositories provide a place to follow development and share improvements.

## News and releases

Our release articles introduce new capabilities and improvements, with links to the people and organizations supporting the project.

The [news archive](/en/news/) is a place to follow the platform over time, from compatibility updates and editing tools to new ways of exploring maps and data.

## From QGIS to the web

G3W-ADMIN publishes and organizes QGIS projects, while G3W-CLIENT provides the browser interface for exploring maps. Optional components extend the platform with tools such as collaborative editing and QGIS Processing.

Projects can retain the styles, forms, relations and print layouts prepared in QGIS. Administrators can organize users and permissions, while map users search, query and work with the information available to them.

The [online demo](/en/about/#demo-3-11) offers a way to try the client with a building and maintenance management project. It includes a public account for exploring geometric and relational editing alongside map search, charts and printing.

## Learn and contribute

The [documentation](/en/docs/) covers installation, administration and the map client. Start with the [Quick Start](/en/docs/quick-start/) for a practical introduction or explore the individual components and their source repositories.

Questions and ideas are welcome in the [community](/en/community/), where users can join discussions, follow the roadmap and find ways to contribute. The [project page](/en/about/) introduces some of the people behind G3W-SUITE.
