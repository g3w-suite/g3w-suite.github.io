# Alessandro Pasotti

![Alessandro Pasotti](https://github.com/elpaso.png?size=400)

**Alessandro Pasotti's public bio jokes about fixing a bug every day; his open-source work follows through with a practical focus on the server side of GIS.**

## Beyond G3W-SUITE

At [ItOpen](https://www.itopen.it), he works with QGIS. His own projects include [QGIS Geocoding](https://github.com/elpaso/qgis-geocoding), [QGIS Server Vagrant](https://github.com/elpaso/qgis3-server-vagrant) and [django-dag](https://github.com/elpaso/django-dag): tools that range from finding data to building and deploying GIS infrastructure.

## In G3W-SUITE

In G3W-ADMIN, he works on the details between QGIS Server and the people administering a WebGIS. One example adds temporary annotation layers to WMS GetPrint output, so printed maps can carry notes drawn on the map. He has also fixed ArcGIS vector-tile services and OpenRouteService integration. [GetPrint change](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin/commit/f1ed598d) · [Vector-tile fix](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin/commit/8634e385).
