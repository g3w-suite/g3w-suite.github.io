Innovative Open Source Geospatial Solutions

G3W-SUITE is an open-source application, highly integrated with the QGIS Server API, dedicated to publishing QGIS projects as WebGIS mapping services. The suite also allows the deployment of collaborative web cartographic systems with shared access.

Thanks to its extreme flexibility and stability, the suite is currently used by many Public Administrations, National Parks, Research Institutes, and professional studios. The application can also be extended through dedicated specialized modules.

We collaborate with GIS companies and professionals to provide the best level of support, deployment, and development. If you are a system integrator or a mapping software company, G3W-SUITE is the ideal solution to add to your portfolio.
